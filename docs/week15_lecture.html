app.py — the model switch and show()
COURSE_MODEL = "Course model, keyword rules if it is down"
KEYWORDS_ONLY = "Keyword rules only (no AI)"


def make_model(choice):
    """Return the model the sidebar asks for."""
    if choice == KEYWORDS_ONLY:
        return KeywordModel()
    return FallbackModel(OllamaModel(MODEL_NAME), KeywordModel())


def show(text):
    """Write text in the page. A $ is escaped so Streamlit does not read it as math."""
    st.markdown(text.replace("$", "\\$"))

Three new tests

The report counts correctly; the edit changes the chosen row; the edit leaves the other rows alone. That makes 15 or more. app.py gets no tests: the autograder runs without Streamlit.

Discussion 1: what staff may change
Which of your records should staff change on the Staff tab, and which should change only through a request?
Look for: a reason tied to who is affected and who is accountable.
Discussion 2: session state
What would go wrong if the chatbot were created outside session_state?
Look for: a new chatbot, with an empty history, on every click.
Discussion 3: the open limit
Who should be able to open your Staff tab, and how would a real business make sure?
Look for: a sign-in, and staff accounts kept apart from customers.
Misconceptions to listen for

“Streamlit keeps my variables between clicks.” Only st.session_state does.

“The chat history is in the database.” It is in st.session_state; a refresh clears it.

“I should test app.py.” The autograder cannot; keep app.py thin.

“Reject deletes the request.” It marks the request rejected.
